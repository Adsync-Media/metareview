## Problem
An explicit `--base main` resolved to main's **tip**, while the default base was `merge-base(HEAD, main)`. Once main advanced, two things went wrong:
- `base..HEAD` pulled main's new commits, inverted, into the reviewed diff.
- `review epic-ready`, `fsm init` and `record-lenses` could disagree on the base, which is why CLAUDE.md carried a "base-consistency rule" workaround.
